# Policy relevance: what the regulatory review establishes

> **Previous-direction draft:** retained as background. Its proposed CM-weight experiment is deferred under the [new MEF-readiness mission](../mef_research/MISSION.md); it is not the next step for the active report.

**Review completed on 6 November 2026; targeted documentary review, not an exhaustive legal opinion.**

The policy question is not simply which OM/BM weighting produces the largest or smallest estimate. It is whether the applicable approach is clearly specified, appropriately justified and transparently applied. FINCAPES examines how consequential these accounting choices are while keeping the question of legal applicability separate from the question of physical accuracy.

The review found that Indonesia already has relevant provisions. Kepmen ESDM 163.K/HK.02/MEM.S/2021 assigns its 50% OM/50% BM columns to mitigation activities other than wind and solar; the 75% OM/25% BM columns are designated for wind and solar. Geothermal therefore falls within the former category in that decree. Its third operative provision also allows factor changes following updated data. This finding does not establish that the same decree or weighting governs every programme or remains applicable throughout the research period.

Permen ESDM 22/2019 identifies responsibilities for collecting information, calculating factors and establishing them. Permen ESDM 16/2022 addresses electricity-sector carbon accounting, reporting and verification. The newer Perpres 110/2025 includes baseline assumptions and methodology in reporting and provides for independent validation and verification. Its transition provisions mean older instruments cannot simply be assumed invalid because the previous presidential regulation was revoked. Permen LH/BPLH 10/2026 adds the current SRUK registry context. Project-specific applicability still requires checking the complete current methodology and implementing rules.

**The preliminary policy implication is to assess implementation and transparency, not to assume that Indonesia has no rules or that 50/50 has been disproved.** A candidate recommendation is a clearly versioned factor register and a project-level disclosure sheet identifying the grid, data years, applicable methodology, weighting, validity period and generation assumptions. Whether these require new guidance, better implementation of existing requirements, or a formal amendment remains to be established.

The next numerical analysis will vary CM weights with generation, schedule and the OM/BM source pair held fixed. It will show the scale of weighting sensitivity, not discover an “ideal” factor. Alternative weights will be labelled analytical scenarios rather than lawful options for claiming credits. A defensible final conclusion may support a targeted improvement, or may find that existing requirements are adequate and that implementation evidence is the remaining need.

Evidence, provision/page references, archived documents and unresolved checks are recorded in `policy_regulatory_review/README.md` and `source_manifest.json`. In particular, the authoritative current geothermal methodology text has not yet been verified; a missing retrieval must not be reported as a missing rule.
